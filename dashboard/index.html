Operational Multi-Sensor Data Feeds

VAJRA operates on a continuous 5-minute multi-sensor data fusion pipeline modeled directly on Indian meteorological observation standards:

  • Doppler Weather Radar (DWR): 5-minute volume scan reflectivity (Composite Max-Z) calibrated to tropical Indian monsoon Z–R relationships (Z = 300·R1.4).
  • INSAT-3D / 3DR Satellite: Thermal Infrared (TIR1, 10.8µm) Brightness Temperature (Tb) detecting rapid cloud-top cooling and deep convection overshooting tops (Tb below −50°C).
  • Ground Lightning Networks: IITM/IMD lightning flash telemetry evaluating total stroke density and 2-sigma acceleration jumps.
  • Orographic Terrain: 1 km SRTM digital elevation grid modeling Himalayan ridge lifting and flash-flood catchment funnels.

Note: The demo engine runs calibrated, physically-governed simulations reproducing historic Indian storm profiles (Uttarakhand Cloudburst, Delhi Squalls, Kolkata Kalbaishakhi) to allow deterministic testing and evaluation.
